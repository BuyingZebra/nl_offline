Physical model and validation


Original reference: 87.5% aperture fill, 0.18 reflector strength, 0.11/0.13 native-pixel reflector displacement, contrast 1, no aging. Corrected shape-driven: the entire STN substrate has an area-averaged 87.5% optical fill; only actual source-artwork boundaries introduce the corresponding inactive-gap colour. This retains the original mean screen colour without drawing cell grids. Shadow overlap follows NOMAD silhouettes at the original offset and strength. Shadow visibility above 1× deliberately exaggerates that calculation for inspection only.


Electrical: reconstructed STN drive with original generated director tables, row+column crosstalk 1.00, 20°C, ionic history, captured row latch, 59.7275 Hz frame reference. The feedback buffer is 32-bit float.


Physical material: director coordinates and ionic charge are integrated each simulated frame by the original reference shader, with continuous artwork drive and a shared RC correction. Uses measured-source lookup tables, 20°C material reference, 59.7275 Hz timing and RGBA32F feedback. Optics: original colour curve and reflective shadow parameters, generalized to overlapping artwork silhouettes. The 160 × 144 original output is retained as a control.
