Arrow Maze

Tap an arrow to send it out head-first. Its winding body follows the same path. The straight exit lane beyond its head must be clear. A blocked tap costs one blue drop.

Every board is generated from a seed and constructed backwards, guaranteeing at least one full solution.

For iPhone: host this PWA over HTTPS, open it in Safari, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
