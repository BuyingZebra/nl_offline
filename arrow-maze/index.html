Arrow Maze

Tap an arrow to send it out head-first. Its winding body follows the same path. The straight exit lane beyond its head must be clear, including its own tail. A blocked tap costs one blue drop.

Start with three simple arrows on Level 1. New turns, interlocking paths, and larger boards arrive gradually as you progress. Each level has a guaranteed solution.
