Reading these maps: Roads, place names, and terrain depicted in the background can be useful as historic references. Overprinted flight lines and photograph ID dots are not roads. These JPGs are generally not georeferenced; coordinate extraction needs registration and comparison with modern GIS before any road could enter navigation.
