NL Offline v0.13
================

Level 1
-------
927 official places and 859,329 origin/destination combinations from the NL Road Distance Database. Official distance/time remains the authoritative trip value.

Level 2
-------
v0.13 changes road-only path selection from shortest distance to a fastest-reasonable model. It gives explicit priority to major Newfoundland corridors, including Route 1 / TCH and Route 75, while assigning lower effective speeds to smaller roads.

The v0.12 GPS matching, ferry safeguards, validated anchor corrections, adaptive ETA, full-screen Follow mode, offline preparation and local road-test logging remain in place.

This is situational navigation, not turn-by-turn navigation. The graph does not encode every legal road restriction or temporary condition.
