NL Offline v0.12 — Road-Test Intelligence Build

LEVEL 2 VALIDATION
- Applies 21 conservative routing-anchor corrections selected from the v0.11 validation audit.
- Corrections are limited to <=2 km movement, <=1% validated median error, <=10% p90 error, and >=5 km median improvement.
- Original anchors remain the source for general community label placement; corrected anchors are used only to enter/exit the routing graph.
- Route Status identifies trips using a calibrated anchor.

LIVE ETA
- Combines the official remaining NL-RDDb time, observed GPS speed, and actual-vs-official pace.
- Live influence ramps up gradually as the app observes more driving, rather than letting one early GPS speed reading dominate the ETA.
- Ferry time remains authoritative and is not incorrectly scaled by road speed.

ROAD-TEST DIAGNOSTICS
- Stores a capped local log of trip loads, drive start/stop, and GPS/map-matching samples.
- Records GPS accuracy, speed, snap distance, route progress, off-route state, and ETA estimate.
- Logs never leave the device automatically. Export Log uses the device share sheet when available, or downloads JSON.
- Clear removes the local road-test history.

DRIVING VIEW
- Adds current speed and GPS accuracy to the driving HUD.
- Full-screen Follow remains available for simulation and live GPS, with less map chrome during driving.

PWA
- Service worker/cache version bumped to 0.12.0 while retaining the safe-update rules introduced in v0.11.
- Completed version caches are now immutable: index.html can no longer refresh alone and become mismatched with older cached JavaScript/data.

MAINTAINABILITY
- Split the monolithic app.js into focused core, map, route-path, route-progress, route-trip, PWA, and GPS modules.
- This keeps future updates smaller and makes routing, rendering, GPS/PWA code easier to audit independently.
